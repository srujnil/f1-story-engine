# Circuit outline assets

The circuit SVGs in this folder come from
[MasterPlay007/F1-Track-Layouts-SVG](https://github.com/MasterPlay007/F1-Track-Layouts-SVG)
and are available under the CC0 1.0 Universal license.

The local copies have only been adapted for browser styling and animation by adding
an element ID, `pathLength`, and `currentColor` stroke.

## Madring and Sepang outlines

`madring.svg` and `sepang.svg` are not in the repository above. They were
generated from the track centrelines in
[bacinger/f1-circuits](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits) (MIT License,
Copyright (c) Tomislav Bacinger) by projecting the GeoJSON coordinates into the
same 500x500 viewBox and applying the same `id`, `pathLength` and
`currentColor` adaptation.
